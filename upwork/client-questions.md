# Client questions — prepared answers

Use in Upwork proposals, Fiverr chats and Telegram replies. Adjust the tone, keep the numbers.

## "What does it cost?"

Depends on scope, but my starting prices: landing page $40–120, Telegram bot $120–200, price parser $60–120. I give you a fixed price before we start, and it does not change afterwards.

## "How long will it take?"

Landing page — 1–3 days. Telegram bot with admin panel — 3–5 days. Price parser with several sources — up to a week. I always tell you the exact deadline before payment.

## "I do not have a technical brief"

That is fine, and honestly it is the normal case. Describe the task in your own words — I will ask the clarifying questions and propose the structure myself. Usually two messages are enough to pin down the whole thing.

## "Can you work with my existing code / bot?"

Yes. Send me the repository or the relevant files and I will work inside your structure, not around it.

## "What do I need to run this?"

For the bot: a Telegram token and your chat id. For the panel: a machine that can run Python 3.9+. No databases, no Docker, no framework. I can deploy it on a VPS with auto-restart.

## "Do I get the source code?"

Always, and it is public on GitHub before you pay the balance. You can read it, check it, and keep it.

## "Do you do revisions?"

Yes. One round of revisions is included in the price, plus a week of support after delivery — small tweaks, wording changes, adding a field.

## "What if it does not work as expected?"

You have the source code and the README from day one, so you are never locked in. I fix what is not right as part of the order.

## "Why are you cheaper than an agency?"

I am a developer, not an agency. No account manager, no overhead, no junior developer doing the typing. You talk to the person who writes the code.

## "Can you do it in Ukrainian?"

Yes. The bot, the panel and all communication can be in Ukrainian.

## Payment terms

50% upfront, 50% after delivery and acceptance. I send the code and the full README before the final payment, so you always have the result in hand.

## Honest note (if they push the price down)

If the budget is under $40, I will tell you honestly what is realistic at that level rather than take the money and deliver something broken. Often the smaller version is all you need.
