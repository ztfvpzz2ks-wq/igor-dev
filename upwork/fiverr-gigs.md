## Gig description

Tired of losing customer requests in a busy Telegram chat?

I will build you a Telegram bot that captures every request automatically — the customer's contact, what they need, and when it came in — plus a clean web panel where you manage everything: search, status changes, CSV export.

Your customers write naturally, in their own words. The bot figures out the rest.

What you get:
- Bot that accepts free-form messages (no rigid forms)
- Auto-detection of the service type and contact details
- Support for Russian and Ukrainian
- Web admin panel with search, status filter and CSV export
- Pure Python standard library — no heavy dependencies, deploys in a minute

Send me a message first and I will tell you exactly what is needed for your business, even if that means a smaller, cheaper solution.
